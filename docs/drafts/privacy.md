# Privacy (draft)

move38 B.V., Poortwachter 81, 1188 CH Amstelveen, the Netherlands, runs mlch.app. Contact: george@m38.ai.

## The mlch app

mlch runs on your Mac. Your canvases, recordings, transcripts and linked folders stay on your Mac. The AI runs through your own Claude Code or Codex, under your own account with Anthropic or OpenAI. Services you add a key for (Groq, Exa, Firecrawl, fal, Gemini) receive what mlch sends them for that purpose, under their own terms. Speech is transcribed on your Mac unless you choose Groq.

## Sharing on mlch.app

When you share, mlch uploads what you share (the canvas, its files, and the content of items its link cards point to) to mlch.app, stored on Cloudflare (R2 and Durable Objects). Anyone with the link can see it. Shared things stay until you delete the link.

To share from mlch.app you sign in with Google. We keep your Google account id, email address and name, when you signed in, and which links you made. We do not get your Google password or anything else from your Google account.

## Payments

Stripe handles payment. We keep your Stripe customer id and your subscription's status and renewal date. Stripe keeps your payment details and billing address under its own privacy policy.

## Other

Cloudflare records requests (address, time, page) for security and to keep the service running. We use no analytics or advertising cookies.

To delete your account and your shared links, write to george@m38.ai.
