Refunds and cancelling

Last updated 13 October 2026

The subscription

The mlch app is free. Sharing on mlch.app costs $12 a year plus applicable tax, charged by Stripe when you subscribe and on each yearly renewal until you cancel.

Withdrawal within 14 days

You may withdraw from the subscription within 14 days of subscribing, without giving a reason, and get a full refund. To do so, email legal@m38.ai from the address you signed in with, or send us any other clear statement. We refund within 14 days of receiving it, to the payment method you used. The subscription then ends: links you made keep working, but you can no longer make new ones.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time in the mlch app (Settings, Sharing, Manage subscription). The subscription then runs to the end of the year you paid for and is not renewed. After the 14 days above, the remaining part of a year is not refunded.

Links you made keep working after the subscription ends. You can no longer make new ones.

Friend codes

A subscription paid with a 100% friend code costs nothing, so there is nothing to refund.
