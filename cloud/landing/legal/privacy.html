The mlch app

The mlch app runs on your Mac and keeps your canvases, recordings, transcripts, files and settings there. We do not receive them.

The AI in mlch runs through your own Claude Code or Codex, under your own account with Anthropic or OpenAI, and under their terms and privacy policies. Services you add a key for (Groq, fal, Exa, Gemini) receive what mlch sends them for that purpose, under their own terms. Speech is transcribed on your Mac unless you choose Groq.

What mlch.app keeps

Only if you share from mlch.app:

  • Sign-in: your Google account id, email address and name, when you signed in, and your sign-in sessions. We do not get your Google password or anything else from your Google account.
  • Subscription: your Stripe customer id, your subscription's status and renewal date, and the friend code you used, if any. Stripe keeps your payment details and billing address; we do not.
  • Shares: what you share, as uploaded by the app (a canvas, its files, and the content of items its link cards point to), and which links you made. Anyone with a link can see what it shows.
  • Server logs: requests to mlch.app and share.mlch.app (IP address, time, address requested, browser), kept for a short time.

People who only open a shared link do not sign in; for them, only server logs are kept.

We use no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

Why

  • Sign-in, subscription and shares: to provide the sharing service you signed up for (performance of a contract).
  • Payment records: because tax law requires them (legal obligation).
  • Server logs: to keep the service secure and running (legitimate interest).

Who processes it

  • Cloudflare hosts mlch.app and stores shares and account data.
  • Stripe handles payments and subscriptions.
  • Google handles sign-in.

These companies may process data outside the European Economic Area, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long

Your account and shares are kept until you delete a share in the app or ask us to delete your account. Stripe keeps payment records for as long as the law requires (in the Netherlands, seven years). Server logs are deleted after a short time.

Deleting your account

Email legal@m38.ai from the address you signed in with. We delete your account, every share you made with its files, your sessions, and your Stripe customer (which cancels the subscription). We keep only a record that a deletion happened and when, not whose.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete and receive a copy of your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Email legal@m38.ai. You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) or the authority where you live.

Changes
